About this planner

Plan a year (4 terms) for one learning area and level. Drag achievement-standard (gold) and content-description (strand-coloured) pieces from the left tray into the grid — rows are the area's strands plus a 🎯 Standards & focus row; drop a piece in as many cells as you need. In Week view you plan one term at a time (the Term focus column holds term-wide pieces). Capability pieces and your own custom pieces come from the other tray tabs. The coverage bar tracks the guideline of ≥ 1 achievement standard per term (≥ 4 across the year). The 🌳 Standards tree below the grid shows how content descriptions build toward achievement standards (per the vault's AS↔CD concordance): placing a standard makes it glow and sets the content that builds toward it pulsing; scheduled content fills in with a ✓. Tree nodes drag into the grid just like tray pieces. Click any piece to read its full verbatim text. Remove a placed piece with its ×. Save downloads the plan as a .json file; Load restores it — the browser itself remembers nothing between visits.
